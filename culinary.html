Gâteau Invisible aux Pommes

A french recipe that is actually easy

September 27, 2026

It is officially fall, thus my favorite fruit is once again the star of the show! I had heard of this recipe before, but never made it, and now I can say this will actually be the only apple dessert I ever eat.

Cake:

  • 4 large apples or 6 regular - I used mutsu which turned out amazing, but you can use any firm and tart apple like granny smith, golderush, etc. Anything else will turn out too mushy.
  • 2 eggs
  • 60 gramd powdered sugar
  • 100 grams whatever milk is in your fridge (I used skim)
  • 20 grams melted (but not hot) butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 80 grams flour (I used gluten-free for my momand it turned out great)
  • 8 grams baking powder
  • Optional: 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  1. Preheat over to 400 degrees F.
  2. Generously butter your pan (I used a springform because it was easier to get out)
  3. Using a mandolin on a thin setting, slice all apples with the skin on.
  4. In a bowl, whisk eggs and powdered sugar.
  5. Mix in melted butter (make sure it is not hot, otherwise it will cook your eggs), vanilla, and milk.
  6. Mix in flour and baking powder.
  7. Layer apples in the pan, it will look like way too many apples, but they will shrink.
  8. Pour batter on top of apples into pan.
  9. Optional: top with a sprinkling of brown sugar for a carmelized look.
  10. Baking for 30-35 minutes. Let cool in pan.
  11. Pop out and serve with dusting of powdered sugar if desired!

Topping:

  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • Powdered sugar to taste (I used about 2 tablespoons)
  • 1 teaspoon apple pie spice
  1. Whip heavy cream until soft peaks.
  2. Mix in powdered sugar and apple pie spice until stiff peaks.
  3. Serve on top of sliced cake.
