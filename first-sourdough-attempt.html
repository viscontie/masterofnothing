Roasted Garlic and Rosemary Sourdough Loaf

...my first super successful loaf!
← Back to Food

Posted on September 28, 2026

This was my second loaf ever and it went so much better than the first. I can't explain the level of giddiness you feel when your loaf comes out well, it's truly addictive and there are going to be many more loafs to come. Also, I can't stress enough how adding just a bit of rye flour to your dough makes it so much better texturally and adds that extra flavor that just takes your loaf to the next level. I'm hoping to slowly start to incorporate more rye into my loafs as I learn and experiment more with my sourdough. Lastly and most importantly, my starter's name is Gerdie, named after this old woman I met in Maine once and was hardie, tough, and independent and I still remember her like 15 years later and i can't think of better traits to have as both a woman and a starter.

Ingredients:

  • 362 grams water
  • 125 grams starter
  • 450 grams bread flour
  • 50 grams rye flour
  • 1 head roasted garlic (I roasted it with olive oil and rosemary a few days before)
  • 6 large springs of rosemary destemmed
  1. Mix water with starter with a fork until fully dissolved
  2. Add bread flour and rye flour and mix until shaggy and fully combined.
  3. Cover and let rest for 1 hour
  4. Perform 4 coil and folds every 30 mins (repeat 4 times)
  5. Cover and bulk ferment 7 hours, ideally someplace warm (70-80 degrees)
  6. Shape dough and put into bowl with clean dishtowel in it and fold dishtowel on top so loaf is fully covered before placing in fridge overnight.
  7. Preheat dutch oven in 500 degree oven for at least 30 minutes
  8. Take loaf out of fridge, score and place into dutch oven immediately
  9. Lower oven to 450, and bake for 30 minutes with lid on
  10. Remove lid and bake for another 10-12 minutes. I bake mine until the internal temp is 210.
  11. Let cool completely before slicing.
